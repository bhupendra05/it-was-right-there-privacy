IT WAS RIGHT THERE: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

IT WAS RIGHT THERE is a single-player puzzle game. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no login, and it does not send your personal data, gameplay or device information to us or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, skill level, Endless best score, collected facts, daily missions and gift streak, and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, clearing the site data in your browser, or using “Reset everything” in the game's settings deletes it. The Android app turns off cloud backup, so this data is not copied to Google backups.

The puzzles

All levels and Endless puzzles are built into the game or generated on your device; nothing is downloaded or uploaded.

Ads and game portals

The mobile app currently shows no ads. When you play IT WAS RIGHT THERE on a game website (for example CrazyGames), that website may show ads or offer its own account and save features under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play or game-portal page (Taletwist Games).
